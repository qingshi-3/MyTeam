Selected original free-sample textures, unmodified. Sources: https://dreams-circle.itch.io/evfx-blazeforge and https://dreams-circle.itch.io/evfx-strike . Original archive inventory is in web/vfx-sample-viewer/assets/provenance.json. Exact copied-file hashes are in provenance.json.

Commercial/non-commercial use and modification permitted; credit required. Assets and derived assets must not be redistributed standalone. These files are integrated game dependencies, not an asset pack. Blazeforge original manual retained. Strike sample erroneously ships the Slash manual; Strike credit/terms verified from its official product page, as recorded in the viewer source inventory.

Fire uses smoke cells, flame flow and distortion. Punch and charge use the directional wind/slash masks. The source multi-hit timeline and background are not reused. Stage choreography and spatial projection are this project's adaptation.
