# Deep Autobattler And Adjacent Mechanics Research

Status: Paused — Checkpoint 51 `mirror-throne` complete

## Goal

Replace the completed store-page-led breadth scan with a genuinely deep, source-triangulated research corpus that can support later system-design discussion. Audit every title in the existing 65-game candidate pool, research the actual rules, builds, player decisions, counters, version history, and reported failure modes of every retained title, and synthesize reusable structures for this project's combat-build systems.

The previous breadth pass remains useful only as candidate discovery. A store or product page may establish that a title exists, its release state, and its public feature claims; it cannot by itself establish how a build works, how players use it, whether it is strong or weak, or why a system succeeded, failed, was nerfed, reworked, or removed.

## Confirmed Solution

- Preserve the existing 65-title breadth scan as an explicitly labelled discovery layer. Demote store-page-derived mechanism records to hypotheses/leads rather than verified mechanism evidence.
- Search every candidate title individually. For each title, seek actual mechanics explanations, guides, build discussions, gameplay analysis, patch/rework history, and player/developer discussion rather than stopping at the store page.
- Create a per-title dossier for every candidate, including titles ultimately rejected. A dossier records what was searched, which substantive sources were found, release/version state, confidence, and either a complete analysis or the reason the title remains discovery-only.
- A released title enters the deep corpus only when its central claims are supported by at least:
  1. one substantive rules/mechanics/manual/wiki or detailed gameplay-introduction source; and
  2. one substantive strategy/build/formation/economy/guide or practical-analysis source.
  A store page, search snippet, mirror, listicle sentence, or autogenerated database entry does not satisfy either requirement.
- For important claims about balance, failure, unreadability, dominance, rework, removal, or player behaviour, seek a third source type such as official patch notes, developer commentary, maintained historical documentation, statistics, detailed reviews, or clearly labelled community discussion. Do not infer causality from popularity, review score, or shutdown.
- Give representative anchor games materially deeper treatment across rules, multiple builds, counterplay, patches, and community/meta interpretation. Long-tail games are not exempt: every retained long-tail title must have enough evidence to explain its distinctive mechanism in use, not merely repeat its marketing promise.
- Normalize every retained game's design through the project's build grammar:

  > driver/engine + state/resource + payoff + survival + spatial condition

  Also record recruitment/economy/pivot decisions, payoff ownership, counters, failure explanation, version dependence, and project compatibility.
- Synthesize across mechanisms only after the dossiers exist. No cross-game design conclusion may rely solely on store/product pages.

### Adaptive Depth Protocol

- Every title still receives the same minimum audit: identity and release state, alias / mode / version map, a search for substantive rules and practical-play sources, a logged route audit, evidence gaps, and an explicit disposition. Adaptive depth changes how far a qualified title is explored; it does not permit silent omission.
- Research depth is decided jointly by system complexity, mechanism uniqueness, relevance to this project, accessible source density, and the amount of meaningful version change. Project relevance is assessed against build-system axes, defense-to-offense or other output conversion, economy / recruitment, space / formation, tactical-command boundaries, enemy counter packages, and failure explanation.
- Large or anchor games are researched through relevant mechanism slices rather than an attempt to exhaust the whole game. A valuable slice should include multiple named, versioned builds where available, their economy / spatial / ownership structure, counters, and lifecycle changes; unrelated modes, eras and content catalogs are out of scope.
- A distinctive long-tail title must at minimum close one build around its defining mechanism: engine, state / resource, payoff, survival, spatial condition, payoff owner, economy, and either a pivot or a counter. If its source package reveals additional materially different structures, expand the dossier until those structures stop changing the design understanding.
- Repetitive, narrow, unreleased or low-evidence titles stop after identity / version mapping, required route audit, and an explicit gap explanation once additional searches have low expected information gain. Do not manufacture builds, multiply mirrors, or retain weak claims merely to meet a count.
- Source and record counts are workload and saturation heuristics, not a uniform quota per title. Every dossier must explain why its chosen depth was appropriate and why research stopped at that point.
- The retention threshold does not change: at least one substantive non-store rules / mechanics source and one substantive non-store practice / strategy source are required, and every deep-evidence record must link at least two substantive sources. Store pages, search snippets, mirrors, uncaptioned titles / footage and inaccessible text do not count; versions and modes must not be blended.

## Authority Impact

- All outputs remain exploratory research, not accepted gameplay or system authority.
- Accepted player-facing build grammar remains in `gameplay-design/combat-build-framework.md`; core rules remain in `gameplay-design/tower-autobattler-core.md`.
- The previous 72-card atlas and current research files remain non-authoritative.

## Scope

### Candidate Audit

- Audit all 65 titles currently indexed by the breadth pass. No title may silently disappear.
- For unreleased, delisted, extremely low-information, or otherwise unresearchable titles, record the queries/source routes attempted and classify them as `discovery-only`, `insufficient-evidence`, or `rejected`; do not count them as deep mechanism evidence.
- Record aliases, regional titles, major mode names, release/closure state, and the observed version/date necessary to avoid mixing eras.

### Per-Title Dossiers

Each dossier must contain, when evidence exists:

- title identity, subgenre, release/live/closure state, observed version or period;
- source list with URL, author/publisher, date, source type, quality, accessibility, and what claim it supports;
- core run/match loop and the real decisions made before and during combat;
- at least two concrete build/archetype examples for retained anchor titles and at least one concrete build example for other retained titles;
- build decomposition: engine, state/resource, payoff, survival, spatial condition, payoff owner;
- recruitment, upgrading, equipment/relic, economy, pivot, replacement, and opportunity-cost structure where applicable;
- targeting, formation, lanes, adjacency, movement, battlefield-object, or item-grid consequences where applicable;
- counterplay, bad matchups, adaptation windows, and how failure is explained to the player;
- relevant patch/rework/removal history and the evidence-backed reason when available;
- known player complaints or emergent behaviour, explicitly labelled as community or secondary interpretation;
- transferable principles, incompatible assumptions, confidence, unresolved questions, and final corpus disposition.

### Depth And Breadth Gates

- Audit: 65/65 candidate titles have dossiers or explicit exclusion dossiers.
- Deep corpus: target at least 40 researchable titles, including at least 20 Steam/independent long-tail titles and at least 8 adjacent-genre titles. If evidence availability prevents a gate, do not lower the standard; report the shortfall and which candidates failed.
- Anchor depth: at least 12 representative titles have six or more substantive non-store sources spanning at least three source types, including rules/mechanics and strategy/practical play; patch/history or community/meta analysis is required when available.
- Other retained titles: at least two substantive non-store sources of different functional types. Multiple pages that repeat the same text count once.
- Corpus sources: target at least 180 distinct substantive non-store pages before saturation review. Continue beyond the target while new sources materially change build understanding, counterplay, failure analysis, or version history.
- Mechanism evidence: target at least 200 normalized deep-evidence records. Near-duplicate effects, generic feature labels, marketing promises, and unsupported inference do not count.
- Negative/reworked depth: include at least 20 evidence-backed cases of nerfed, reworked, removed, dominant, misleading, unreadable, non-functional, or rejected mechanisms across materially different games. Commercial failure and mechanical failure remain separate claims.

These gates prevent shallow completion; they are not permission to pad the corpus. Evidence quality and explanatory completeness outrank raw counts.

### Required Mechanism Dossiers

Cross-game synthesis must deeply cover:

1. shield, armor, health, healing, mitigation, retaliation, shield generation/break/consumption, and defense-to-offense conversion;
2. elements/affinities, statuses, reactions, DOT, control, propagation, detonation, resistance, and cleansing;
3. summons, corpses, death triggers, sacrifice, inheritance, temporary units, occupancy, and population counting;
4. attack/spell cadence, mana/energy, criticals, targeting, focus fire, ramping, sequencing, recursion, and rate limits;
5. formation, adjacency, range, facing, lanes, body blocking, movement, displacement, battlefield objects, and item grids;
6. traits/classes/factions/tags, breakpoints, vertical/horizontal investment, bridge units/items, and traitless alternatives;
7. equipment, relics, augments, artifacts, rule rewrites, stat conversions, payoff ownership, and opportunity cost;
8. population, shop/recruitment, rerolling, reserve/bench, replacement, upgrading, economy, rewards, and pivoting;
9. enemy packages, elite modifiers, bosses, telegraphs, counter design, adaptation windows, and failure explanation;
10. deterministic ordering, readability, combat reports, source attribution, scaling caps, recursion guards, and anti-stalemate rules.

### Source Strategy

Use and distinguish:

- official manuals, rules, databases, patch notes, developer blogs, interviews, talks, and support pages;
- maintained wikis and historical databases;
- detailed written or video guides, build guides, tier discussions, Steam Community guides, GameFAQs-style guides, and strategy sites;
- gameplay reviews, postmortems, statistics sites, and well-evidenced forum/Reddit/Steam discussions;
- transcripts or timestamped video evidence when written documentation is unavailable.

Community sources may establish observed strategy, sentiment, or emergent behaviour but never silently replace official rules. Record disagreements and version drift. Search results are discovery routes only; read the underlying page or accessible transcript. Never bypass login, paywall, CAPTCHA, robots restrictions, or removed content.
