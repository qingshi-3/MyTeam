用户否定上一轮 A／B／C 区别过小，要求放开设计。本轮不再把差别限定为材质／线宽：分别探索实物挂件、星仪符号和漫画版画，改变图标本体、技能形体、装备孔与数字主次。保留已基本认可的信息位置、左右无底板属性、底部姓名和框外招募；仅出静态方案，未改运行代码或素材。

内置 `image_gen` 每版独立调用，参考当前正式展开截图 `.godot/ui-review/unit-card-disclosure/offers-hover.png`；右侧是样本放大，不是新增侧栏。前轮图片保留，不当作已选方向。

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create a genuinely new, bold fantasy game UI art-direction proposal. This is not an incremental icon reskin. The previous proposals failed because all had the SAME generic shield/star/droplet glyphs and five identical little bronze squares. DO NOT repeat that approach.
Input image 1 is a REFERENCE for the left red-haired archer character and general information locations ONLY. It is NOT an icon or small-control style guide. Use only that archer, not the other two characters. Keep her identity, pose and costume substantially unchanged. Keep a broad thick gray-stone outer frame and full-height character artwork; keep the face unobstructed. You may radically redesign all inner icon silhouettes, relative emphasis, typography, skill outlines, equipment receptacles and small decorative treatments. Design a coherent art system rather than assorted stickers.
Information architecture to keep: two skills and exactly three equipment sockets, all at the TOP inside the frame, not at the bottom. Left and right attribute columns inside the frame, icon+number only, NO per-attribute backing rectangles, pills, medallions or little panels. A shape that IS the icon is welcome, but no extra enclosing tile behind each attribute. Bottom ends with the name ribbon "连弩手", attack 22 at its left, health 155 at its right, spell power 0 just above it. Portrait runs all the way behind this ribbon, no blank lower band. Recruit action "招募 · 3 金币" stays OUTSIDE below the frame. No aggregate side panel and no extra buttons.
Retain 8 side attributes per side, all zero values included. Left values in order: 1, ×1, 5.2, 0%, 150%, 0%, 0%, 0%. Semantics respectively defense, attack speed, range, critical chance, critical damage, lifesteal, dodge, control resistance. Right values: ×1, 60, 20, 5, 10, 50, 20, 0. Semantics movement, max mana, starting mana, time-based mana, on-attack mana, on-damage mana, per-hit mana cap, healing. You are expressly encouraged to INVENT NEW understandable silhouettes for these concepts instead of tracing the reference's generic symbols. Distinguish related mana glyphs structurally, not just by hue. Full labels only in a tooltip, never clutter beside every value. Skill semantics: volley of arrows and precision shot. Equipment remains three empty positions; don't invent equipped items.
Output one landscape 1536x1024 design presentation: roughly left half is the large COMPLETE card and external action; right half shows generous enlarged samples of the actual proposed attribute symbols and numbers, the exact same two skills+three equipment receptacles, and one small individual-hover tooltip headed "暴击率" with sentence "普通攻击触发暴击的概率。". Right side is a sample board, not attached UI: label it "细节放大". Small section labels allowed, no long explanatory prose. Match actual proposed card graphics in the enlarged samples. Clean intentional editorial presentation appropriate to this specific art direction. No browser chrome, logos, watermark.
Preserve the user's accepted general card structure. Be adventurous in formal design, visual hierarchy and craftsmanship. No oily highlights, chrome, bloom, large glowing haze, decorative filigree, microscopic detail or noisy grunge. Not a generic asset-store icon pack.
EXACT TITLE: "D · 战利品挂件".
RADICAL DIRECTION: tactile, chunky storybook inventory objects, not symbols printed on flat squares. The icon IS a freestanding tiny physical object. An irregular wooden-and-iron buckler for armor, a miniature cluster of fletched arrows for speed, a carved sight/arrow for range, a chipped arrowhead for critical chance, a splintered impact shard for critical damage, a small crimson heart-shaped flask for lifesteal, a leather boot for dodge, broken iron links for resistance. Mana family: little ceramic flasks with restrained turquoise contents and distinct carved neck/base silhouettes and integral notches, simplified enough to read small. Broad warm clay-like painted planes and soft contact shadows, simplified hand-carved forms. Natural leather tan, iron gray, warm bone, tiny muted turquoise and red accents. No smooth candy gems, no flat generic droplets. Off-white chunky serif numbers sit immediately beside these objects with a clear baseline; they are not labels on plates.
TOP DESIGN MUST BE NOT FIVE SQUARES: the two skill controls are irregular bronze-and-carved-bone insignias shaped like a compact fan of arrowheads and a circular sight, larger than an attribute glyph. A controlled visual gap separates three clearly empty U-shaped leather loops/harness sockets with iron clasps and small plus marks. The objects seem attached near the inner upper edge; do not add a full-width strap or toolbar. Three empty equipment receivers are equally usable but each is open-centered, not a boxed black tile. All five reside above the face, wholly readable. Keep topology two skills + three gear.
The tooltip resembles one small folded parchment note with an uncluttered dark title, minimal edging, not a stone modal. The section arrangement on the sample board can feel like a curated tabletop catalog, but not messy: confident whitespace, beautifully aligned enlarged artifacts. This direction should immediately read as physical adventure equipment, not digital vector glyphs. Frame and portrait still dominate.
```
